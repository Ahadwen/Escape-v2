# Cards — draft

Status: **first draft for discussion.** Names, numbers and effects are all placeholders to react to.

## Structure

| Tier | Ranks | Count | Role | How you get them |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | 2–6 | 20 | Stats | Chests (common) |
| T2 | 7–10 | 16 | Effects | Chests (rare), safehouse recipes |
| T3 | J, Q, K | 12 | Strong effects | Safehouse recipes only |
| Aces | A | — | Out for now | — |
| Jokers | — | wild | Stand in for any one recipe ingredient (recipe costs more gold) | Chests (rare) |

- **5 card slots.** Picking up a card (which also gives gold) opens the inventory: swap, rearrange or discard.
- **Recipes** are fixed per card (learnable, like League's item tree): exact cards, with flexibility where useful (e.g. "any ♥"). The safehouse offers a random few recipes each visit, so holding components for a recipe that may appear is the core decision.
- **Set bonuses** for holding several cards of one suit (thresholds TBD).

## Suits

| Suit | Family | T1 stat pool |
|---|---|---|
| ♥ Hearts | Survivability | Max HP, armour, elemental resist, HP regen |
| ♦ Diamonds | Wealth scaling | Gold gain, shop/recipe cost, interest |
| ♣ Clubs | Crowd control | Will (ability power), CC duration, knockback power |
| ♠ Spades | Mobility | Move speed, cooldown reduction |
